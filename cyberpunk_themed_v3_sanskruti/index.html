About Hackspiration '26

Hackspiration '26 is a premier hackathon organized by Microsoft Learn Student's Club at VIT Pune. This 26-hour offline event brings together the brightest minds to innovate, create, and compete across three cutting-edge domains: Cloud, AI, and Web3.

With a total prize pool of ₹1,00,000, participants will tackle real-world problem statements, network with industry leaders, and showcase their technical prowess in an electrifying game-show style format.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hackspiration '26?

+

Hackspiration '26 is a 26-hour offline hackathon organized by Microsoft Learn Student's Club at VIT Pune, bringing together developers to innovate across Cloud, AI, and Web3 domains.

Who can participate?

+

Students from any college or university can participate. Teams can have 2-4 members. Check the registration page on Unstop for detailed team formation rules.

What is the prize pool?

+

The total prize pool is ₹1,00,000 distributed across three tracks: Cloud (₹35,000), AI (₹40,000), and Web3 (₹25,000). 2-3 winners will be declared under each domain.

Do I need to pay to participate?

+

No, participation is completely free! We provide free meals, swag bags, and all necessary resources during the event.

What should I bring?

+

Bring your laptop, charger, valid ID proof, and enthusiasm! We'll provide everything else including food, beverages, and workspace.

How will teams be selected?

+

Teams will be shortlisted based on their registration applications. Shortlisted teams will be announced on the website before the event.

Shortlisted Teams

Shortlisted teams will be announced soon. Stay tuned!

Winners

Winners will be announced after the event. Good luck to all participants!
